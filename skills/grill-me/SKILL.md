name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a non-codebase plan or design (talks, articles, proposals, external specs, life decisions) until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a non-code plan, get grilled on a proposal, or mentions "grill me" in a non-code context. For plans targeting an existing codebase use `domain-model` instead.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
